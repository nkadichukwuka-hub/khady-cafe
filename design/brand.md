# Brand

Brand-level direction. For type, color, and UI rules see `style-guide.md`.

## Personality

Warm, neighborly, a little rustic — like a small-batch roaster's zine. Not
corporate, not precious.

## Voice

- Friendly and direct. Short sentences. Sentence case everywhere.
- Talk about people and place, not "artisanal experiences."
- Example: "Fresh pastries out of the oven by 7. Come early."
- Full voice rules (CTAs, errors, empty states): `style-guide.md` §1.

## Wordmark

- Set as "khady's café" in **Fraunces italic, lowercase**.
- Clear space: at least the cap height of the "k" on all sides.
- Minimum width: 96px on screen.
- Do not stretch, recolor outside the palette (`espresso` on light,
  `cream` on `bean`), or add a drop shadow.
- Source files in `assets/logo/`.

## The stamp

A secondary mark: a circular rotating seal (ring text + "Est. 2017" center).
Used as a hero flourish or a section end-mark, one per page. It is the only
place the brand uses all-caps letter-spaced type, because it reads as a
maker's seal. See `components/content.md#stamp`.

## Photography

- Natural light, real food, real regulars.
- Warm white balance. Avoid heavy filters.
- Show hands, steam, imperfect plating.
- On product shots, crop to a `4/5` portrait on a `latte` background.

## Iconography

- Simple line icons, 1.5px stroke, rounded caps and joins, no fills.
- Hand-drawn in `components/icons.tsx` — no icon library.
